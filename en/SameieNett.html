SameieNett
a digital foundation for residential buildings.

What if a condominium association could own and understand its digital infrastructure in the same way it owns other shared facilities? SameieNett brings networking, Wi‑Fi, security, access, cameras, IoT and technical operations together in one scalable architecture.

Segmentation & security

A building should not be one large home network.

A standardized VLAN model makes it possible to separate homes and services, simplify troubleshooting and create clear security boundaries.

VLAN Name Purpose Principle
10 SPS-MGMT UniFi, switches, APs and administration Restricted
20 SPS-BEBOER Internet for residents / home segments Isolated
30 SPS-TV-ALLENTE TV, set-top boxes and IPTV Service
40 SPS-GUEST Guest network and temporary access Separated
50 SPS-DRIFT-TEKNISK Technical room, energy system and gates Operations

FAQ · networking in residential associations

Questions boards and developers actually ask.

What is SameieNett?

SameieNett is a concept for standardizing networks and digital infrastructure in shared residential properties, with centralized operations, segmentation and the ability to add more services.

Why use VLANs in a residential building?

VLANs can separate administrative systems, residents, guest networks, TV, cameras, IoT and service access. This creates clearer security boundaries and easier troubleshooting.

Is this only a Wi‑Fi solution?

No. Wi‑Fi is one part. The architecture is intended as a foundation for access, cameras, technical systems, energy, sensors and automation.

Can the concept be used in existing buildings?

Yes, but physical cabling, fiber, technical rooms and apartment cabinets must be mapped. A site survey is required before a real solution can be engineered.

Can this be planned during new construction?

Early design is precisely when structured cabling, technical rooms, AP placement, cameras and access can be coordinated most effectively.
