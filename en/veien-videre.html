A NEW ROUTINE

From consulting to a new role.

My path into IT went through NæringsAkademiet and then Bergen Technical College. At BTF, theory quickly became practice: toward the end of the two-year programme I began running the college's IT environment, and after graduating I continued there as an employee for around six months. From there I moved on to itslearning. In 2011 I entered consulting through ID Scandinavia, beginning around 15 years across several consulting firms, most recently blinQ. Now I am moving into a planner role at Statnett – a shift from consulting to being part of one organisation with a long-term public mission.
