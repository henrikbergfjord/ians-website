Curiosity needs direction.

How can we help children understand the technology they grow up with?

I think we need more than bans. Children need present adults, clear limits on screen time and space to explore together. They should not have to work everything out alone.

Learning to distinguish real content from AI-generated images and claims, checking sources and understanding what we share are part of this. Ground rules must match age and maturity and evolve with technology.

Today’s children may build tomorrow’s solutions. I do not have all the answers, but I want to use IANS to ask questions, learn and explore a safer digital everyday life.
