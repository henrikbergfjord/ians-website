THE PROCESS

Idea → build → test → learn.

01

Notice a need

A problem, an irritation or a question becomes the starting point.

02

Build something

Make a working version and see what it can do.

03

Use it

Real use reveals what is missing and what should change.

04

Keep developing

New needs lead to new features, and the lessons carry forward.
