01 · INTELLIGENCE

A smarter Siri experience

Apple continues moving Siri toward more natural conversations and better understanding of context. The goal is an assistant that can understand what you mean without requiring perfectly phrased commands.

What you're seeingAn IANS concept illustration of the new intelligent-assistant experience.
Why it mattersLess time learning commands and more natural interaction with your iPhone.
Siri
How can I help?
Understanding context…
WHAT'S NEW IN iOS 27 Siri AI is designed to be substantially more conversational and context-aware than traditional Siri. Apple says it can understand personal context, onscreen information and broader requests, and can perform actions across supported experiences.
REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES
  • Ask a question naturally, then continue with follow-up questions.
  • Ask about supported information currently visible on your screen.
  • Use Siri to help find information from supported personal content.
  • Use supported Siri AI capabilities to perform actions instead of navigating manually.
HOW TO USE IT
Activate Siri→Speak naturally→Ask follow-up→Take action
COMPATIBILITY & AVAILABILITY Siri AI requires an Apple Intelligence-compatible device and supported language. It starts in English. Important for European users: Apple says Siri AI is not initially available on iPhone or iPad in the EU with iOS 27.
🇪🇺 EU availability: Apple says Siri AI is not initially available on iPhone or iPad in the European Union with iOS 27. Apple currently gives no launch date for Siri AI on iOS in the EU.
IANS TIP If you are in the EU, distinguish between Siri AI and other Apple Intelligence features. iOS 27 does not mean that every Siri AI feature shown by Apple is currently available on your iPhone.

02 · CAMERA

Visual Intelligence

Your camera increasingly becomes a way to understand the world around you. Visual intelligence can help identify what the camera sees and surface useful information or possible actions.

What you're seeingA camera view analysing an object and presenting contextual information.
Why it mattersThe camera becomes more than a way to take pictures — it becomes an input for information.
Camera
Visual Intelligence
🌿
Object recognised · More information
WHAT'S NEW IN iOS 27 Visual Intelligence uses the iPhone camera and Apple Intelligence to help you understand things around you. It can work with businesses and places, text, plants and animals, products and other visible objects.
REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES
  • Point the camera at a business to view useful information.
  • Translate, summarise or have visible text read aloud.
  • Identify plants and animals.
  • Search for visually similar objects or products.
HOW TO USE IT
Open Visual Intelligence→Point at subject→Choose action→View result
COMPATIBILITY & AVAILABILITY Requires an Apple Intelligence-compatible iPhone. Activation methods vary by model and can include Camera Control, Action Button, Lock Screen or Control Center. Some capabilities vary by language and region.
IANS TIP Try it on things where visual context actually helps: signs, menus, plants, products and places. You will quickly learn which actions save you time.

03 · PHOTOS

More intelligent photo tools

Photos continues to gain smarter editing and organisation tools. AI-assisted features can help clean up an image, improve composition and make it easier to work with your photo library.

What you're seeingAn IANS illustration of intelligent photo editing and reframing.
Why it mattersUseful edits that once required dedicated software become easier directly on iPhone.
Photos
Smart editing
✦ Enhance · Reframe · Clean Up
WHAT'S NEW IN iOS 27 iOS 27 expands Apple's intelligent photo-editing direction. Supported Apple Intelligence devices can use intelligent editing tools to make more advanced changes while keeping the workflow inside Photos.
REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES
  • Remove distracting elements from supported photos.
  • Improve framing with intelligent editing tools where available.
  • Use Apple Intelligence-assisted photo features without moving the image to a separate editor.
  • Keep the original photo available while experimenting with edits.
HOW TO USE IT
Photos→Open a photo→Edit→Choose available intelligent tool
COMPATIBILITY & AVAILABILITY Advanced intelligent editing requires compatible Apple Intelligence hardware. Individual tools can vary by device, language, region and software version, and server-based features may have usage limits.
IANS TIP Use AI editing for corrections rather than automatically changing every image. Small distractions and framing problems are often where these tools are most useful.

A more useful web experience

Safari continues to improve how tabs, websites and information are organised. Smarter browsing tools are designed to reduce clutter and help you get back to the pages that matter.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES

05 · FAMILY

More help for kids and parents

Family and child-safety controls continue to become more useful. The direction is clear: children should be able to explore technology while parents retain practical tools for permissions, communication safety and screen-time boundaries.

WHAT'S NEW IN iOS 27 Apple continues expanding family and child-safety controls in iOS 27, including tools around communication, web access, Screen Time and age-appropriate experiences.
REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES
  • Use Screen Time to create schedules and limits.
  • Review communication-safety protections.
  • Manage web access and supported requests from children.

HOW TO USE IT
Settings→Family→Select child→Review parental controls
COMPATIBILITY & AVAILABILITY Available controls depend on the child's account setup, age, Family Sharing configuration, country or region and current software versions.
IANS TIP Technical restrictions work best together with conversation. Use parental controls to create boundaries, but also teach children why a warning or restriction exists.

06 · PERFORMANCE

Faster everyday iPhone use

Some of the most valuable operating-system updates are the ones you stop noticing: faster actions, smoother transitions, better resource management and improvements to sharing and media handling.

What you're seeingAn IANS performance illustration rather than a benchmark.
Why it mattersA mature iPhone can feel better even when the biggest improvements happen behind the interface.
Performance
Optimised for iPhone
⚡
Faster · Smoother · More efficient
WHAT'S NEW IN iOS 27 Apple says iOS 27 includes significant performance work across everyday iPhone use, including faster app opening and faster loading of new photos on supported devices and workloads.
REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES
  • Apps can feel quicker to launch.
  • New photos can appear faster in supported workflows.
  • Transfers and connectivity improvements can reduce waiting in everyday use.
  • Performance work benefits ordinary interactions rather than being a single feature you have to enable.
HOW TO USE IT
Update iPhone→Restart after update if needed→Keep free storage→Use normally
COMPATIBILITY & AVAILABILITY Performance gains vary by iPhone model, battery condition, storage, workload and other factors. Apple's headline measurements should not be interpreted as a guaranteed speed increase for every action on every iPhone.
IANS TIP Do not judge a major iOS update only during the first hour after installation. Background indexing, Photos processing and other post-update work can temporarily affect battery life and performance.
