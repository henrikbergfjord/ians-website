IANS PICKS

Five generations. Different winners.

🏆

Best overall

iPhone 18 Pro. The newest performance, camera and AI platform.

🪶

Lightest

iPhone 15 Pro. At 187 g, its titanium redesign remains remarkably light.

💰

Smart used-value zone

iPhone 15 Pro / 16 Pro. Modern USB-C and Apple Intelligence support without needing the newest generation.

📷

Big camera-era shift

17 Pro → 18 Pro. The Pro Fusion system and newer tele/front-camera hardware move photography forward.

🔌

Biggest everyday convenience

15 Pro. USB-C ended the need for a separate Lightning cable and brought USB 3 data transfer.

🤖

AI dividing line

15 Pro and newer. The 14 Pro sits on the other side of Apple's hardware requirement for Apple Intelligence.

About this comparison: Technical specifications are based on Apple's published specifications for the Norwegian market. “Best”, “Major jump”, value recommendations and upgrade verdicts are independent IANS editorial assessments. Battery figures are Apple's stated video-playback figures and actual battery life varies with use, configuration, network and other factors. Feature availability can also vary by region and software version.
